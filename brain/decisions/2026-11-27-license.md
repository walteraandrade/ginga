# License: MIT OR Apache-2.0

**Chosen.** Both crates are licensed `MIT OR Apache-2.0`, with `LICENSE-MIT` and `LICENSE-APACHE` at the root. The copyright line names "The ginga authors". Issue #1, pull request #15.

**Options.** Waltinho said any open-source license was fine and left the choice to an agent. The usual Rust pair was the one the issue suggested. A single license (MIT alone, or Apache-2.0 alone) was the other option.

**Why.** It is what most Rust crates use, so whoever depends on ginga already accepts it. Apache-2.0 matches `gpui-pre`, which the adapter links. The pair gives users Apache's patent grant and MIT's brevity, whichever they need.

**Door.** One-way once anything is released under it; two-way before that.
